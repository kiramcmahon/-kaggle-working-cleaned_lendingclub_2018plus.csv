# Customer Payment Behavior and Risk: A Business Intelligence Case Study

This project uses a cleaned subset of Lending Club loan data (2018 and newer) to analyze customer repayment behavior and identify high-risk borrower segments. The goal is to deliver business intelligence insights through KPIs, risk segmentation, and interactive dashboards, helping financial analysts and decision-makers monitor default trends more effectively.

---

## 📊 Project Summary

Using key variables such as loan purpose, credit grade, income level, debt-to-income ratio, and loan status, I explored which borrower profiles are most associated with loan defaults. I created new fields including:

- **Income Tier** (Low / Mid / High)
- **Repayment-to-Income Ratio (RIR)**
- **High-Risk Flag** based on low income, high DTI, and charge-off status

The cleaned dataset includes only the most relevant fields needed for business intelligence analysis, enabling a streamlined dashboard experience in Tableau.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Visualize patterns in defaults by purpose, grade, and income tier
- Create KPI cards and risk dashboards for executive review
- Simulate the type of insights a BI or risk analyst would deliver to leadership
